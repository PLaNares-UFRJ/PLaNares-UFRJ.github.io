The purpose of the PLaNares group is to develop materials and conduct research in the fields of PLN and computational linguistics. The group is in its early stages and consists of undergraduate and graduate students from the Language and Literature and Computer Science programs at UFRJ, reinforcing the interdisciplinary nature of the field. It was structured so that participants can choose a line of interest and organize themselves to conduct research, apply, and evaluate tools related to that topic.
